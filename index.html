PCA Monterey Bay Region Logo: Usage Guide

The official logo of the Monterey Bay Region, Porsche Club of America, approved unanimously by the Board of Directors on October 26, 2026.

A red Porsche 356 drives toward a California live oak, with the Carmel Valley and Big Sur ridgeline and Monterey Bay beyond.

Vendors: use only these files. They replace all earlier versions, including any image marked EXAMPLE.

Use and permissions

This is the Monterey Bay Region's own logo. The region may use its authorized PCA and Monterey Bay Region logos but must not use artwork outside the permissions already granted. Vendors may use these files only to produce approved region items. Please do not stretch, recolor, crop, retype or add effects to the logo, and do not add Porsche AG marks such as the Porsche crest or script.
